# Das Reich ohne Raum Translation Project

## Project Goal

Create a faithful, **literal** English translation of Bruno Goetz's *Das Reich ohne Raum* (The Kingdom Without Space, 1919) - a German mystical novel analyzed by Marie-Louise von Franz in her *Problem of the Puer Aeternus* lectures.

## Translation Philosophy

**Reader Experience First**: The goal is to experience the book as close to the original German reader's experience as possible - as *literature*, not as a psychological case study.

### Approach

1. **Literal over Interpretive**: Preserve Goetz's actual word choices, imagery, and poetic voice
2. **von Franz as Consultant**: Use her English retelling as reference, but don't adopt her interpretive liberties
3. **Preserve Atmosphere**: Maintain the mystical, dreamlike quality of the original German prose
4. **Structural Fidelity**: Mirror the exact structure of the German source file

### What to Avoid

- Adding modern psychological framing
- Smoothing over Goetz's intentionally strange or archaic phrasings
- Inserting words not present in the original (e.g., von Franz added "Vikings" where the German has none)
- Losing dedications, titles, or structural elements

## Book Structure

- **VORWORT** (Foreword by Walter R. Corti)
- **IN MEMORIAM FERRUCCIO BUSONI** (Dedication poem)
- **AN FO** (Poem to the character Fo)
- **ERSTER TEIL: DIE BOTSCHAFT** (Part One: The Message) - Chapters 1-6
- **ZWEITER TEIL: DIE WANDERUNG** (Part Two: The Wandering) - Chapters 7-10
- **DRITTER TEIL: DIE TAT** (Part Three: The Deed) - Chapters 11-14
- **DAS REICH** (The Kingdom) - Chapters 15-18

## Translation Notes

### Poems

The two opening poems are crucial for setting the tone. von Franz translated them but lost:
- The dedication titles (IN MEMORIAM FERRUCCIO BUSONI, AN FO)
- Specific imagery: "Rauch" (smoke), "springende Bronnen" (springing fountains), "brausend" (roaring)
- The formal sonnet structure of the first poem

### Terminology

| German | Literal | von Franz | Notes |
|--------|---------|-----------|-------|
| Reich ohne Raum | Kingdom without Space | Kingdom without Space | Keep literal |
| Ewiger Knabe | Eternal Boy | Eternal Youth | "Boy" is more direct |
| Athermeer | Ether-sea | - | Compound word, keep hyphenated |
| Gestalt | Form/figure | shape | Context-dependent |
