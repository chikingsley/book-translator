# 1919 Edition Evidence Checklist

Purpose: verify that the 1919 witness files (`DE-1919`, `EN-1919-canonical`) match the physical 1919 first edition structure.

## How to use

1. Open the physical 1919 book.
2. For each checkpoint below, mark `PASS` or `FAIL`.
3. If `FAIL`, note the page and what differs.

## Structural checkpoints

1. Front matter has title page + copyright + contents.
2. No `VORWORT` / no foreword by Walter R. Corti.
3. No `AN FO` poem.
4. No `KOMMENTAR` sections by M. L. von Franz.
5. Dedication poem heading is `AN FERRUCCIO BUSONI` (not `IN MEMORIAM`).

## Part/chapter checkpoints

1. Part One exists and contains 6 chapters:
   - Schimmelberg
   - Die Begegnung
   - Fo
   - Die Entfesselten
   - Die Wunder
   - Vor der Entscheidung
2. Part Two exists and contains only 1 chapter:
   - Das offene Tor
3. Part Three exists and begins immediately after Part Two with:
   - Stuhlbrestenburg
4. Final section (`Das Reich`) contains exactly 4 chapters:
   - Die Verwandlungen der Liebe
   - Der Untergang
   - Die Wiederkehr
   - Letztes Kapitel: Der Schneewanderer

## End-matter checkpoint

1. `Weltbühne Radium` appears as an in-chapter subsection under the final chapter (not as a separate edition-level appendix).
